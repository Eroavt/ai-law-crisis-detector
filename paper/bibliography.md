# Bibliography: UZH §4.4 format

> **Formatting note for Athira:** apply SMALL CAPS to authors' surnames in Word per UZH §4.4. The entries below are in alphabetical order by surname and follow the conventions in the *Guidelines for Academic Essays* §4.4 (independent works: author, title, edition, place, year; dependent works: author, title of article, reference).
